## Outstanding scientific limits

- FIRMS points provide detections, not clear-land observation coverage; no clouds correction or confirmed absence of fire is inferred.
- VIIRS CSV `version=2` means Collection 2. It is not the monthly production-software version. The documented Type bug cannot be established or ruled out from that field alone; the original source-granule reprocessing version remains unverified. Type-filter sensitivity is in each models.json.
- MODIS labels 6.2 / 6.03 / 61.03 are retained verbatim. Their exact historical encoding has not been established. The calibration matches the earlier label, but that does not prove stationarity.
- The publication is a detected-activity proxy, not burned area, incident counts, or a forecast. Cell-level harmonized values are estimates; arbitrary fine polygons are not certified by country-level accuracy.
- Cell support consists of locations with at least one accepted archived thermal detection in any of the four sensors, including all source types. Outside that support a query returns unknown; it does not establish zero fire.
- Administrative assignment uses cell centers and simplified boundaries. Unassigned centers remain in an explicit bucket and in national totals; they are not silently snapped to a department/state.
- 2023–2025 is a drift stress test, not evidence that the pre-2012 relation held unchanged.
- No 2026 observations are included. 2000–2002 raw sensor counts are retained separately, with null harmonized values.
